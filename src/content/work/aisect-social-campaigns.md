## 01 — The brief

Plan and run social media campaigns across Instagram and Facebook for a
skill development organisation. Content calendars, campaign copy,
creative concepts, and coordination with the creative team producing the
assets.

## 02 — The context

Seven campaigns in seven months means the bottleneck is almost never
ideas. It is the handoff. A campaign concept that lives in the
strategist's head produces four rounds of revisions and a creative team
who reasonably feel they are guessing.

Two platforms with genuinely different behaviour did not help — the same
concept needs different execution on Instagram than on Facebook, and
pretending otherwise produces content that is slightly wrong in both
places.

## 03 — The observation

The campaigns that moved fastest were not the best-conceived ones. They
were the ones where the brief was specific enough that the designer did
not have to interpret.

"Make it engaging" produces a revision cycle. A concept with a stated
purpose, a defined format, a copy direction and a reason the audience
would stop scrolling produces something you can react to on the first
pass.

## 04 — The insight

For an in-house social function, brief quality is a throughput problem
before it is a creative one. Most of the time lost in a campaign is lost
between the idea and the asset, not inside either.

## 05 — The strategy

- **Concept and calendar ahead of the month**, so campaigns had room to
  be built rather than improvised.
- **Briefs written to be built from** — purpose, format, copy
  direction, and the reason someone would stop.
- **Platform-specific execution** of shared concepts rather than one
  asset cross-posted.
- **Monitor and adjust in-flight**, using performance to decide what got
  extended and what quietly ended.

## 06 — The work

- 7 social media campaigns across Instagram and Facebook
- Content calendars and campaign copy
- Creative concepts developed in Canva as the starting point for the
  creative team
- Creative team coordination and briefing
- Ongoing performance monitoring across both platforms

## 07 — The result

**7 campaigns delivered** across the period, with **400+ follower growth**.

Follower growth is a soft number and I will not oversell it. The result I
would actually point to is throughput: seven campaigns in seven months,
across two platforms, with a small team and no backlog at the end of it.

## 08 — What I learned

That being the strategist means being responsible for how buildable your
idea is, not just how good it is. I got noticeably better at writing
briefs during this role, and it made everything downstream faster.
