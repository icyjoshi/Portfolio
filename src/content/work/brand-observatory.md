## The plan

Four industries — the shortlist is currently travel, lifestyle, fashion
and entertainment — read across three business models: B2B, B2C and D2C.
The final brand list is not fixed yet.

Every brand goes through the same lens: positioning, audience, value
proposition, brand voice, content, acquisition, conversion, retention,
competitive advantage, marketing gaps, strategic opportunities.

## Why it is not a research report

Because a research report ends at "here is what they do", and that is the
least interesting sentence available. The framework is **observe →
interpret → recommend**, and the recommend step is compulsory. If a
section cannot end in something I would actually advise doing, it does
not go in.

The output is meant to be editorial and visual — closer to a magazine
feature than a deck.
