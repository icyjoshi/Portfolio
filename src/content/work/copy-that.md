## The format

**Before → After → Why.**

The "why" is not optional and it is not a paragraph of self-congratulation.
It names the specific problem in the original — buried offer, feature-led
headline, a claim the reader has no reason to believe — and what the
rewrite does about it.

## What goes in it

- Landing page rewrites
- Ad copy
- Campaign taglines
- Brand voice makeovers
- Product positioning
- Email
- Social copy

Where the original belongs to a real brand, it is analysis of public
work and it is labelled as such. Nobody commissioned any of this.
