## The format

Every entry answers the same four questions:

1. **What I noticed** — something specific, not a summary of the campaign
2. **Why it matters** — the interpretation, which is the part most
   commentary skips
3. **What I would do** — an actual alternative, not a critique
4. **What I would test** — because an opinion you cannot test is just a
   preference

## Where it lives

These publish in **The Lab** rather than as full case studies. They are
meant to be quick — the point is frequency and honesty about what I
notice, not depth.
