## The plan

One brand, invented, built end to end:

**Research → audience → insight → positioning → value proposition →
brand voice → visual direction → campaign → content → acquisition →
paid media concept → landing page → email → retention.**

## Why

Because the interesting part of brand work is not any single stage — it
is whether a decision made in the positioning survives all the way to the
email at the other end. That is the thing almost no portfolio piece
actually shows, because it is the part that takes months.

This will be the longest project on the site, and it should be.

In progress. Category chosen, research underway.
