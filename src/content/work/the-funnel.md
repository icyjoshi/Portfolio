## The honest version

I hold the Google Digital Marketing & E-Commerce, Google AI, Meta Social
Media Marketing and Amazon Ads Foundation certifications. I understand
how Google and Meta Ads are structured, and I can reason about audience,
creative and measurement.

What I do **not** have is significant hands-on paid media spend behind
me. So this project is filed as a developing capability, and it will keep
that label until the label stops being true. If you need someone who has
managed real budget at scale, that is not yet me — and I would rather
tell you now than after you have hired me.

## The plan

**Ad → landing page → conversion → email → retention**, with:

- A defined audience and the reasoning behind it
- The funnel drawn out, stage by stage
- Explicit hypotheses — the things that would have to be true
- Creative variants and CTA options
- A measurement framework
- A/B testing ideas, prioritised by what would change a decision
